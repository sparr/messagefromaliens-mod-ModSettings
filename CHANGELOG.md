# Changelog

## Unreleased

## [0.2.0](https://github.com/sparr/messagefromaliens-mod-ModSettings/releases/tag/v0.2.0) - 2026-11-22

- Added unlock messages: mods can announce a gated feature in the game's own sentences, in chapter explanations, on win count pages, and in the left panel at the win.
- Added `|` (or) and `+` (and) in gate tokens, and win count pages that only mods fill.
- Added `Api.IsChoiceGlyph` for other mods.
- Long labels are cut to 12 glyphs, and a setting with many choices lists them on a page of their own.
- Fixed enum choices, text field editing, mods that share a glyph name, and mod pages being taken for game pages or quizzes.
- Faster page layout.

## [0.1.0](https://github.com/sparr/messagefromaliens-mod-ModSettings/releases/tag/v0.1.0) - 2026-11-18

- Initial release: other mods' settings on the game's settings page, in glyphs, with text fields, quoted values, pages of their own, and gates.
